Lesson 3

How to talk to AI

Takes about 5 minutes

AI works better when you give it clear instructions — the more specific you are, the better it can help you.

Instead of
"Make a post."
Try this
"Create a friendly Facebook post promoting my homemade ube cake for Filipino families. Keep it short and include a call to action."
Remember: tell AI the what, who, why, and how.

Do this now
